\section{Identification of Technical Risks}
\label{sec:technical_risks_identification}
Throughout the development of HUGO, key technical risks were identified by evaluating the likelihood of occurrence $p$ and severity of impact $s$ \cite{Lanzafame2024}. Both of the criteria were evaluated on a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest score and 5 being the highest. Details regarding the allocation of both scores are explained in \autoref{tab:risk_map_scores}.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
        \caption{Likelihood/Severity scores used in risk assessment \cite{MITRECorporation2012}}
    \begin{tabular}{|c|c|p{12cm}|}
    \hline
        \textbf{Score} & \textbf{Likelihood} & \textbf{Severity} \\ \hline\hline
         1 & $0\%< p<1\%$ & Negligible \\   \hline
         2 & $1\%<p<30\%$ & Marginal (insignificant impact, can be managed by applying standard procedures) \\   \hline
         3 & $30\%<p<50\%$& Considerable (significant impact, can be managed by additional/contingency procedures) \\   \hline
         4 & $50\%<p<70\%$& Critical (possible injury or minor damage to property or HUGO, has to be addressed by contingency/emergency procedures) \\   \hline
         5 & $70\%<p<100\%$& Catastrophic (possible death or serious injury, severe damage to property, loss of HUGO, has to be addressed by contingency/emergency procedures) \\   \hline
    \end{tabular}
    \label{tab:risk_map_scores}
\end{table}

Operation risks refer to hazardous scenarios which can occur both on the ground and in the air. According to the JARUS guidelines for risk assessment of UAVs, they can be identified and classified based on the following criteria \cite{Jarus_main}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The extent to which third parties or property on the ground could be endangered by the activity;
    \item The complexity, performance and operational characteristics of the unmanned aircraft involved;
    \item The purpose of the flight, UAS type, probability of collision with other aircraft, class of airspace;
    \item The type, scale, and complexity of the UAS operation or activity, including, where relevant, the size and type of the traffic handled by the responsible organisation or person;
    \item The extent to which the persons affected by the risks involved in the UAS operation are able to assess and exercise control over those risks.
\end{itemize}

The tolerance level of both development and operational risks can be determined by analysing the corresponding risk matrix (\autoref{fig:initial_risk_matrix}). Based on the position of each risk in the matrix, it can be assigned one of the following tolerance levels:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Low/controlled (green):} The combined likelihood and severity of the risk make it acceptable, and their impact of the project is deemed as acc.
    \item \textbf{Moderate/tolerated (yellow): } The combined likelihood and severity of the risk make it tolerable under the condition that appropriate preventive measures are applied and contingency procedures are clearly outlined and followed in case the risks manifests itself.
    \item \textbf{Extreme/unacceptable (red): } The combined likelihood and severity of the risk make it impossible for the design or operations of HUGO to proceed. Unless extensive preventive measures and contingency procedures are developed and adhered to, the success of the project cannot be ensured.
\end{enumerate}
