The operation of an aircraft will inevitably lead to the generation of noise. This can be a great nuisance for area surrounding the area of operation of the aircraft. Therefor it is important to obtain an understanding of the not only the magnitude but also at what frequencies dominates the noise-spectrum. Noise is small pressure differences which travels through a medium to the receiving end. For an aircraft the primary source of noise would be the engine.
