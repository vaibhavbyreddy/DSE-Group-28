\chapter{Verification \& Validation}
\label{ch:V_and_V}

\todo[inline]{From the \textit{Project Guide}\\ The Validation \& Verification Procedures enter the project in two ways:\\ In the first place all used numerical and/or computer models need to be verified (according to the definitions and procedures covered in the course AE3205 – Simulation, Verification and Validation) before these models can actually be used during both the conceptual and detailed design. The verification procedures and results need to be included in both the Midterm and the Final report.\\ In the second place, the Validation \& Verification Procedures describe how the proof will be obtained that the product or system meets the requirements imposed on it. It distinguishes between test, analysis, simulation, review of design (documentation) and similarity (i.e. a comparable design has been developed and operated successfully) as methods to obtain that proof.}
