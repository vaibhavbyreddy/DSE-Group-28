\chapter{Project Design \& Development Logic Diagrams}
\label{app:project_design_development_daigrams}
This appendix contains the diagrams illustrating the post-DSE development of project HUGO mentioned in \autoref{ch:preliminary_design_summary}. \autoref{fig:gantt_chart} showcases the future development Gantt Chart covering the preliminary design, detailed design, prototype construction and testing, manufacturing and certification, operation, and end-of-life solutions. The project design and development diagram is shown in \autoref{fig:proj_design}, and the associated cost breakdown structure is presented in \autoref{fig:cost_breakdown}.

The Gantt Chart shown in \autoref{fig:gantt_chart} was broken down into the development phases specified in \autoref{sec:pdd_logic}, and the resolution was adjusted from one month to one quarter to half a year depending on the required level of detail for each phase. Direct dependencies between the tasks have been indicated with the use of colours, where progressively darker values suggest that a task shall be performed once the once before it has been completed (or commenced, if concurrent completion is envisioned).
