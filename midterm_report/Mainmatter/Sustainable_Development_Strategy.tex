\section{Sustainable Development Strategy}

\subsection{Environmental sustainability}
To access the environmental impact of the system, a preliminary LCA-analysis was set up using the open-source software openLCA. Contrary to the streamline used for the material selection the preliminary LCA will take flight operations, maintenance and the recycling of the material will be taken into account.

\subsection{Social sustainability}
Great care has been put into this project to ensure that the production and operation of the aircraft is performed in such a way that no harm comes to anyone both physically and mentally. This is achieved by continuing with the approach introduced in the baseline review (insert section here) and by adhering to the regulation stipulated by the European commission regarding the emission and noise generated during  flight (see \autoref{sec:environmental_protection}).
