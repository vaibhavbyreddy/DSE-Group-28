\section{Environmental sustainability}
\label{sec:Environmental sustainability}
To access the environmental impact of the system, a preliminary LCA-analysis was set up using the open-source software openLCA. Contrary to the streamline used for the material selection the preliminary LCA will take flight operations, maintenance and end-of-life of the material into account. The files for the preliminary LCA can be found at  \footnote{\href{https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/jeu8lkwe8dxdl074q1pb4/DSE28PreLCA.zip?rlkey=sbr04scgz3ccazsg1z8u35c1m&st=hamfk6k7&dl=0}{Preliminary LCA}}

Having identified these it is possible to focus on the processes which has the greatest influence on emissions. In this case it is apparent that it is the operation of HUGO (the burning of fuel) and the production of the fuel. This should be the  focus for the subsequent development of HUGO to implement methods to mitigate the fuel-burn emission and find production methods of SAF with lower emissions.    

\section{Social sustainability}
\label{sec:Social sustainability}
Great care has been put into this project to ensure that the production and operation of the aircraft is performed in such a way that no harm comes to anyone both physically and mentally . This is achieved by carefully planning the operation of the aircraft and ensuring safety for all stakeholders involved  (see \autoref{ch:operations_logistics}) and by adhering to the regulation stipulated by the European commission regarding the emissions and noise generated during  flight (see \autoref{sec:environmental_protection}).
