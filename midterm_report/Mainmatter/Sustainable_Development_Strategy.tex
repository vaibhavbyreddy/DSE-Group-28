\section{Sustainable Development Strategy}

At this point the design is defined in enough detail that it is possible to make a more detailed analysis of the impact of HUGO.     
\subsection{Environmental sustainability}
To access the environmental impact of the system, a preliminary LCA-analysis was set up using the open-source software openLCA. Contrary to the streamline used for the material selection the preliminary LCA will take flight operations, maintenance and end-of-life of the material into account.

Having the preliminary LCA and using the IPCC 2021 (ISO 14067) method it is possible to calculate the GWP  of the entire lifespan of HUGO:$3.86059\cdot10^{6}$ [kg CO2 eq]. The five largest contributes are shown in \autoref{fig:bigfive} below.   
\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.75\linewidth]{figures/IPCC.png}
    \caption{The five biggest contributes to the overall emissions}
    \label{fig:bigfive}
\end{figure}
Having identified these it is possible to focus on the process which has the greatest influence. In this case it is apparent that it is the operation of HUGO (the burning of fuel) and the production of the fuel. This should be the greatest focus for the subsequent development of HUGO to identify methods to mitigate the fuel-burn emission and find production methods of SAF with lower emissions.    

\subsection{Social sustainability}
Great care has been put into this project to ensure that the production and operation of the aircraft is performed in such a way that no harm comes to anyone both physically and mentally. This is achieved by continuing with the approach introduced in the baseline review (insert section here) and by adhering to the regulation stipulated by the European commission regarding the emissions and noise generated during  flight (see \autoref{sec:environmental_protection}).
