This chapter presents the operational and logistical framework required to bring HUGO into service following completion of the design and certification process. The operational analysis describes the intended use of the system, covering the concept of operations, mission objectives, mission phases, operational constraints, maintenance provisions, and future scalability. The logistics analysis addresses the supporting resources needed for sustained operation, including ground personnel, deployment logistics, facility and location requirements, consumables, spare parts, and supply-chain considerations. This combined analysis establishes how HUGO can be practically deployed, supported, and maintained throughout its operational life.
