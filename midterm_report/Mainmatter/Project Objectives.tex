\chapter{Project Objectives}
\label{ch:project_objectives}

The project objectives are formulated according to the SMART method: they are specific, measurable, actionable, relevant, and time-bound. They serve as a roadmap for planning and execution, defining the tangible deliverables and outcomes required within a project’s timeline, budget, and scope.

\section{Performance Objectives}
The performance objectives focus on the results and effectiveness of the project.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item The initial HUGO fleet shall comprise of five aircraft to ensure continuous operational availability across 100 operational days per year with 4 flight tests per day \footref{url:baseline_report}.
    \item The selected configuration shall withstand manoeuvre load factors ranging from −3 to +6 (\autoref{sec:flight_envelope}).
    \item The aircraft shall enable continuous flight and collection of flight test data at 0.75 Mach for at least 5 minutes \footref{url:baseline_report}.
    \item The selected configuration shall carry sufficient fuel for the nominal mission of 30 minutes and four landing attempts \footref{url:baseline_report}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Business Objectives}
The business requirements align with the strategic goals of market entry and client acquisition.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The Netherlands shall serve as the primary market for initial operations, with Den Helder Airport as the designated base of operations \footref{url:baseline_report}.
    \item HUGO shall be capable of serving customers from at least four market segments: universities and academic laboratories, research centres, aerospace companies, and defence organisations \footref{url:baseline_report}.
    %\item The design shall support rapid reconfiguration between client-test campaigns to minimise aircraft-on-ground time between contracts.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Compliance Objectives}
The compliance objectives ensure adherence to regulations and standards.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Each HUGO configuration shall comply with the environmental performance requirements set out in Annex~III of Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 of the European Parliament and of the Council (\autoref{sec:environmental_protection}).
    \item Each HUGO configuration shall comply with the the CS 23 Aerobatic Airplanes requirements and Special Condition for Light Unmanned Aircraft Systems - High Risk (\autoref{sec:CS-23 and SC-UAS}).
\end{enumerate}
