\chapter{Project Objectives}

The project objectives are formulated according to the SMART method: they are specific, measurable, actionable, relevant, and time-bound. The objective of this stage of the project is to establish the final configuration for consideration in the detailed design phase. This is accomplished through preliminary sizing, aerodynamic and structural analyses, together with the evaluation of material selection and manufacturing processes.

The project objectives are split into five main categories: financial, quality, performance, business, and compliance. 

\section{Financial}
The financial objectives focus on budget management.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item The transportation and storage cost of the aircraft shall not exceed \textbf{TBD} (check Den Helder hangar renting). 
    \item For each landing, a fee of \EUR{23.00} shall be paid.
    \item A fee of \EUR{10,170} shall be paid for the initial certification, followed by 12 monthly fees of \EUR{11,050}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Quality}
The quality objectives define standards for the deliverables.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Data shall be downloaded and stored efficiently after each test flight.
    \item A HUGO configuration shall undergo a thorough inspection of all internal and external systems and the external structure before and after each test flight.
    \item A HUGO configuration shall undergo 50 days of maintenance per year.
    \item The maximum relative difference for verification analysis shall be less than 1/%.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Performance}
The performance objectives focus on the results and effectiveness of the project.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item The selected configuration shall be operational 100 days per year with 4 flight tests per day.
    \item The selected configuration shall withstand the constraining loads specified in the flight envelope \autoref{sec:flight_envelope}.
    \item The selected configuration shall satisfy all constraints defined in the matching diagram, including take-off and landing field lengths not exceeding 1275 m.
\end{enumerate}
